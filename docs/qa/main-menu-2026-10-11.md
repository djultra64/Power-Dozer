## Graphics boundaries and outstanding limits

The shadow repair clears the complete sampled texture while preserving the
original 63×63 silhouette clip. Prior original/candidate GPU evidence found
rows 0–62 byte-identical and exactly the final 64 border texels corrected at
native size, with zero borders also at scaled 4K. The texture is RGBA16_UNorm.
The exact user's live 4K baseline symptom was not reproduced in the presented
baseline; these results do not claim that reproduction.

Ticker medal sprites now use the same full-width 16:9 region as the text,
with preserved UV span and edge clipping. Prior actual guest/RT64 fixtures and
native wing captures establish all three present medal types plus rank. Three
matched 4:3 ticker crops were pixel-identical. The absent fourth medal is covered
by the generated-producer fixture, without a native fourth-medal save. There
is no live-aspect feature and no original-ROM emulator comparison.

The integrated candidate also ran an unobscured dozer scene through actual
1920×1080 and 3840×2160 window/fullscreen/window transitions with the silhouette
retained and no displaced shadow-border line in reviewed images; timer exit 0.
An earlier scene run received unrelated controller input while navigating the
map and was rejected. Its repeat isolated nonvirtual devices within the test
process. These captures supplement the causal texture evidence, without
claiming the exact user's baseline reproduction.

The final integrated ticker run recorded 180 process-alive captures (90 per
extent) through fullscreen changes, then exited 0 via Close Game. Reviewed
native images show bronze/silver/gold/rank on both sides with complete sprites
preserved across the former 4:3 boundaries. At 4K the selected full right-rank
image spans the old edge; it is not evidence of that particular sprite lying
wholly beyond it. Earlier 720p full-wing evidence remains the separate causal
check. An initial recording ended before its final 4K frames; those black
captures are rejected despite the timer's normal exit.
